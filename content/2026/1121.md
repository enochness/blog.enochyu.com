Hey, it's been a while since I wrote my last post. If you weren't here
for a while, you may have noticed many changes in this blog, and yes,
I am here to share all about what happened and why I decided to write
again.

Definitely, I think there are pros and cons of writing blogs. If you
don't mind, let me first start with cons. If you'd rather have the
good news first, you can skip a few paragraphs and return whenever
you are ready for bad news. Jokes aside, there were many reasons why
I was hesitant to write for the past few months. First is that it is
time consuming.

Writing a blog or an essay takes time, and I know no matter how long
I spent on a paragraph, I would never be satisfied with it. Also it
doesn't feel very rewarding given the amount of the time you spent on
it. The second reason is privacy. I think this would become a
bottleneck for many others as well because virtually anything on the
internet should be considered permanent. Say you wrote something in
the past. If you hate it now, then too bad, there is no going back.
The time it takes to write and privacy concerns held me back in
writing blogs, and I think it applies the same for many of you reading
this right now as well.

Now while I was gone, I had been writing
[LibreNotebook](https://librenotebook.enochyu.com/). In one sense,
it is just notes on what I learn. In another sense, it is a learning
blog! I write what I learned, but most importantly, I write what I
think. You may have noticed if you read LibreNotebook, but I don't
use LLMs when writing them. Everything there, literally the entire
content, is written by me (or another human) with no AI generated
texts. In other words, I had been writing a lot! As I continue to
write LibreNotebook, I began to notice how my writing changes and
becomes more polished. I also noticed how my thoughts began to change.
The more I write, the more organized my thoughts became, basically
[refactoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_refactoring) my brain.

Besides writing a huge amount of content every day, I also have been
reading a lot. Some of my favorites are...
* [Life is Short](https://paulgraham.com/vb.html) - Paul Graham
* [Everything is Fertile](http://nickcammarata.com/writing/everything-is-fertile) - Nick Cammarata
* [If math is more than proof, we need to better celebrate the rest of it](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/09/18/if-math-is-more-than-proof-we-need-to-better-celebrate-the-rest-of-it/) - Grant Sanderson
* [Write Like You Talk](https://paulgraham.com/talk.html) - Paul Graham
* [for html programmers](https://motherfuckingwebsite.com/) - Unknown

Aside from these, there are so many great essays that I have read that
inspired me to write again. After all, reading essays and blogs before
going to sleep gave me new perspectives that I wouldn't have obtained
otherwise.

Anyhoo I decided to write again because it helps me organize my
thoughts and to perhaps provide useful insights for the readers
just as I got them by reading essays and blogs. And I think those
benefits prevails over cons of being time consuming and privacy
concerns. Yeah so until the next post, have a great day and I will
see you later :))
